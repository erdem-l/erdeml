abstract: "Report from a one-month CNARC fellowship at the Center for Polar and Ocean Studies, Tongji University, Shanghai (April 2025). Based on fieldwork including 20 semi-structured interviews with Chinese academics and experts, and on Chinese-language Arctic literature, the report examines how Chinese Arctic policy discourse seeks a dialectical balance between respecting China’s position as a non-Arctic state and remaining engaged in Arctic affairs, covering science, shipping, governance and Sino-Russian cooperation."
