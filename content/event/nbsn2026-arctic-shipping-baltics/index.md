Panel 2, *Arctic-Baltic Nexus*, at the conference **The Nordic-Baltic Security Nexus: Implications for Canada**, presented by the Eastern Europe Transatlantic Network (EETN) and the North American and Arctic Defence and Security Network (NAADSN). The conference was held in person and online.

The conference examined the growing cooperation between the Nordic and Baltic states, hybrid or "grey-zone" threats, and Canada's role in the region, including Operation REASSURANCE and NATO Multinational Brigade Latvia.

**Speakers**

- Cordelia Buchanan Ponczek, Research Fellow, Finnish Institute of International Affairs: "From Localised War Economy to EU and Allied Responses: Finland and the Nordic Approach to Preparedness"
- Dr. Andreas Raspotnik, Director, High North Center for Business and Governance, Nord University: "The EU and the Arctic: Europe's Northern Neighbourhood"
- Erdem Lamazhapov, PhD Research Fellow, Fridtjof Nansen Institute: "Arctic Shipping: Consequences for the Baltics"
- Dr. Phillip Hughes, Director, DNATO

**Moderator**

- Dr. Ryan Dean, Assistant Professor, Dallaire Centre of Excellence for Peace and Security, Canadian Defence Academy
